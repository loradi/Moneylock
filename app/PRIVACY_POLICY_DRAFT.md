Moneylock is a personal-finance planning app. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Information stored on your device

Moneylock stores the information you enter or create in the app on your device. This can include transactions, merchant names, categories, budgets, income, subscriptions, savings goals, app settings, and Vector conversation history.

The Vector model runs on your device. Vector conversations and model files remain on your device and are not sent to Moneylock for processing.

## Optional private-server sync

Sync is optional and disabled by default. If you configure a server URL and API key in Settings, Moneylock sends transactions, plans, categories, subscriptions, savings-goal information, and related settings to that server to provide the sync you request. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain.

## Device permissions

- **Camera:** used only when you choose to scan a receipt.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** used only when you choose voice entry.
- **Notifications:** used, if enabled, for budget and subscription reminders.

Moneylock does not use these permissions for advertising or tracking.

## Third parties and tracking

Moneylock does not include analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. The on-device model is downloaded from Hugging Face when you request it; no financial records or Vector prompts are sent with that download.

If you configure optional sync, the server you select receives the data described above. Its operator is responsible for its own privacy practices.

## Retention and deletion

You control your on-device records. You can edit or delete records in Moneylock and remove the app to erase its local data. If you enabled optional sync, delete any synced records from that server according to its operator’s process.
